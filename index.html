About Healthify

Food made to make you feel good

Healthify is a healthy food ordering platform built around clear nutrition and simple ordering. We keep meals balanced, label what is in them and let you decide.

  • Vegetarian, vegan and high protein options are marked on every dish.
  • Calories and macros are listed per serving, so you can plan your day.
  • Prices are in rupees and shown before you add anything to your cart.
Questions

Frequently asked

How do I place an order?

Add meals to your cart, open the cart and choose Proceed to Checkout. Enter your name, an optional table number and whether you are dining in or taking away.

Where do the nutrition numbers come from?

They are approximate values per serving, written for this demo menu. Use them as a guide and check with the kitchen if you have allergies or medical needs.

Do you offer vegetarian and vegan food?

Yes. Use the Veg, Vegan and High protein filters above the menu to narrow the list.

Does my cart stay if I close the page?

Yes. Your cart and latest order are saved in this browser, so they are still here when you return on the same device.

Is this a real ordering system?

This site is a working demo. Orders are tracked on this page only and are not sent to a kitchen or charged to a card.
